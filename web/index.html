IGT Layout Planner

Plan a Snow Peak Iron Grill Table setup in 3D, to the millimetre — and know it will actually go together. Every part sits where its real hooks, brackets and slots put it, and the planner tells you what doesn't fit, what the manuals forbid, and what the whole kit weighs.

Getting started

  1. Pick a frame from the library on the left (or start from the one already there).
  2. Click a frame in the view: blue dots mark every edge something can hook onto, and the slots inside take burners and trays. Click a dot to see what fits there.
  3. Read the Build panel on the right: the parts you'd carry, their weight, and anything to fix.

Each tab is its own design, with its own scene. Share sends a link that carries the whole design; Photo makes a picture of it.

Your designs

Everything stays in this browser: no account, no server copy, no tracking. Clearing the site's data clears your pages — use layouts → export to keep a file. A share link carries the design inside the link itself.

The data

Dimensions, weights, the 250mm unit grid and the attachment rules are compiled from Snow Peak's public product pages and assembly manuals; every derived rule cites its source. Where the planner cannot confirm something it says so rather than guess. This is an independent project, not affiliated with Snow Peak; product names are used only to say what each part is.

Open source

MIT licensed — source on GitHub. Found a part that's wrong, or a combination it should allow? Open an issue.
